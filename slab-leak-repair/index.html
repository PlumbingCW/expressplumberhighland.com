Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Highland, CA

We pinpoint the leak under the concrete and offer repair paths that fit your home and budget.

Call (909) 505-3225All Services

Slab leak repair in Highland, CA fixes a water line leaking under your home's concrete foundation. These leaks are hard to see, so people notice warm floor spots, a spinning meter or a surprise bill first. Call to confirm and locate the leak.

If you suspect a slab leak, shut off the main water valve and see whether the meter stops. If it does, the leak is in your system. Call us with the meter reading and any warm or wet floor areas.

Signs of a Slab Leak

These signs often show up together.

  • Warm spot on the floorA hot water line is leaking below.
  • Sound of running water with everything offWater is moving in the pipes.
  • Meter spins with fixtures offWater is leaving your system.
  • Damp carpet or flooringWater is rising through the slab.
  • Higher water or gas billHot water is lost and reheated.
  • Cracks or shifting near the slabWater can wash out soil.

Foundations and Soil in Highland

Highland sits on an alluvial fan at the foot of the San Bernardino Mountains, and homes here are mostly built on concrete slab foundations. Soil shifts and aging copper lines under the slab are common contributors to slab leaks.

East Valley Water District reports average hardness of 152 mg/L, and hard water can add scale that narrows pipes over time. If you have had one slab leak, ask us to evaluate the rest of the system. See repiping if the lines are failing.

Pressure testing and rerouting follow the plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and a repair that changes pipe routing may need a permit and inspection.

What a Leak Does if Ignored

Water under a slab can erode the soil that supports it, lift flooring and feed mold in the subfloor and walls. It also raises your water bill every month.

A hot water slab leak wastes energy too, because the water heater keeps heating water that escapes. Early detection saves both.

Protecting the Home While You Wait

If you suspect a slab leak, close the main valve and lift valuables off the floor. Turn off the water heater if the leak is on the hot line, since it will keep heating water that escapes. Open a faucet at the lowest point to relieve pressure.

Photograph wet or warm areas and note the meter reading. These details help a plumber find the spot and help your insurer understand the loss.
