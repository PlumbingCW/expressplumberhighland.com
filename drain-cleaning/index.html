A clogged drain that pushes water back into a sink, tub or floor drain is an emergency in Highland, CA, so call right away. Drain cleaning means removing the blockage from the pipe with a cable, a jetting hose or a camera-guided tool, then proving the line flows freely before we leave.

While you wait for the plumber, stop running water anywhere in the house, close the toilet lids and keep chemical cleaners out of the drain. A pipe that is already full holds those chemicals in place, and they can burn whoever works on the line next.

Signs the Clog Is Bigger Than a Slow Sink

One slow drain usually means a local clog. A pattern across the house points to something deeper in the line.

  • Several fixtures gurgleAir is being pulled through traps because the branch or main line is restricted.
  • Water rises in the lowest drainA shower or floor drain fills first when the main line is blocked.
  • Toilet bubbles when the washer drainsTwo fixtures share one blocked path downstream.
  • Sewage smell near a floor drainWaste is sitting in the line instead of moving.
  • The clog returns within weeksRoots, scale or a belly in the pipe is catching debris again.
  • Slow drains in every roomThe main line is partly closed, often with grease or roots.

Where the Clog Sits Decides the Fix

A fixture trap or tailpiece

The curved trap under a sink collects hair, soap and food. A cable or a hand-cleaned trap solves most of these in one visit. This is the cheapest clog to clear and the most common.

A branch line

A branch carries one bathroom or the kitchen to the main. Grease, wipes and mineral scale narrow it a little at a time, so water slows for months before it stops. A cable with the right cutting head usually restores it.

The main line

When every drain backs up, the blockage sits in the main line or the lateral to the street. Roots, collapsed pipe and heavy grease live here. We scope the line when the cause is unclear, because clearing a pipe that is broken only buys a few weeks.

Kitchen Sink Clogs and the Disposal Problem

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and coats the wall, then catches rice, coffee grounds and eggshell. After a few months the opening is the size of a pencil.

If your sink has a disposal, the clog is often just past it, and the disposal itself may not be at fault. A plumber checks the disposal outlet first, then cables the line toward the wall. Our garbage disposal repair team handles both parts in one visit when the disposal is jammed too.

Drain Clearing Methods Compared

No single tool fits every blockage. This is how the common methods differ.

MethodBest forLimits
Hand-cleaned trapSink or tub that drains slowlyDoes not reach past the fixture.
Drum cable (snake)Hair, soap and paper in branch linesOpens a channel but may leave a coating on the pipe wall.
Cable with a cutting headRoots and heavy buildup in 3 to 4 inch linesNot suited to cracked or broken pipe.
Hydro jettingGrease, scale and repeat clogsNeeds sound pipe and a cleanout or access point.
Camera inspectionClogs that keep returningShows the cause, does not clear it by itself.

A cable opens a clog. Jetting scours the pipe wall so the clog does not rebuild as fast. We recommend the lighter method first and move up only when the evidence calls for it.

Highland Water, Old Pipes and Why Drains Slow Down

East Valley Water District reports an average hardness of 152 mg/L in its 2026 water quality report, which falls in the hard range. Hard water reacts with soap to leave a scummy film that thickens inside bathroom drains over the years. If your tub drains slower each season, that film is a likely contributor, and it is one reason periodic cleaning beats waiting for a full stop.

The pipe material under a building depends on when it was plumbed and what has been replaced since. Rough interior walls in older cast iron or clay pipe catch debris faster than modern plastic. A camera look after a second clog tells you whether cleaning is enough or the line needs repair.

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and that code sets standards for traps, vents and cleanouts that decide how a drain can be cleared.

What Happens on a Drain Clearing Visit

The visit follows the same order whether the clog is at a sink or in the main line.

  1. Find the blocked section

    The plumber tests fixtures and checks the cleanout to learn how far the clog sits from the house.

  2. Quote before work

    You hear the method and the price before any cable goes in the pipe.

  3. Clear and flush

    The line is cleared, then run with water until it drains at full speed.

  4. Confirm and explain

    We tell you what caused it and whether a camera inspection or cleaning schedule makes sense.
