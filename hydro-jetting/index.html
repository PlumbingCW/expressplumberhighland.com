Emergency Hydro Jetting in Highland, CA

High-pressure water clears grease, scale and roots from sewer and drain lines, with a camera check before and after.

Call (909) 505-3225All Services

Hydro jetting in Highland, CA uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of a drain or sewer line, and it is the right move when a clog keeps coming back. Jetting clears lines, including emergency backups that cannot wait for a regular appointment.

If your main line is backing up, stop using water and keep the toilet lids down until help arrives. Do not pour drain cleaner in, because it can splash back when the jetter opens the line.

What Jetting Does That a Cable Cannot

A cable punches a hole through a clog. Hydro jetting washes the pipe wall clean, removing the grease, scale and silt that give new clogs a place to catch. The result is a line that flows like it did when it was newer.

Jetting uses a hose with a nozzle that sends water backward to pull the hose forward and forward to scrub the wall. Pressure is set to the pipe material and condition, which is why a camera look before jetting matters.

When Jetting Is the Better Call

A cable is fine for a first clog. These situations favor jetting.

  • The clog returns every few weeksThe wall still holds buildup.
  • A cable gets stuckThe blockage is heavy or the pipe is narrowed.
  • Grease in a restaurant or kitchen lineJetting removes grease a cable only smears.
  • Roots in a clay or older lineJetting clears roots and flushes debris, though it does not stop regrowth.
  • Sewage odor from floor drainsBuildup is holding waste in the pipe.
  • Recent cable service failedA different method is needed.

Why Highland Lines Collect Buildup

East Valley Water District's 2026 report lists average hardness at 152 mg/L, so hot water leaves scale and soap residue in lines over time. Older cast iron or clay drain lines have rough walls that hold debris, and a camera look shows which kind of line you have.

Jetting helps both conditions. It removes the soft buildup in newer pipe and clears the heavier scale in older pipe, though a badly deteriorated cast iron line may need repair instead. A camera tells us which case you have.

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which California's plumbing code is based on, lists approved drain pipe materials, and the pressure we use depends on which of those materials your line is.

Our Jetting Process

Jetting is done in stages so the pipe is protected.

  1. Camera inspection

    We look at the line to confirm it is sound and find the clog.

  2. Set pressure

    Pressure is matched to the pipe material.

  3. Jet the line

    We run the nozzle through and back, scouring the wall.

  4. Final camera check

    We confirm the line is clear and show you the footage.

Commercial and Restaurant Lines

Restaurants and businesses with kitchen lines need jetting more often than homes, because grease builds quickly. Regular jetting keeps lines open and avoids a weekend backup.

Our commercial plumbing team can set a schedule that fits your business and note the findings after each visit.
