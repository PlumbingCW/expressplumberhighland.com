Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Highland, CA

Spot repair, pipe lining or full replacement of the line from your house to the street, with a camera to confirm the damage.

Call (909) 505-3225All Services

Sewer line repair in Highland, CA starts with finding exactly where the line failed, then fixing that section with the least digging that will hold. Sewage backing up into tubs, toilets or a floor drain is an emergency, so call right away.

If sewage is coming up, stop running water, keep people and pets out of the wet area and do not use bleach or drain cleaner. Call us, and we will scope the line and tell you what repair makes sense.

Signs of a Failing Sewer Line

A line that is breaking rarely fails all at once. These are the signs that come first.

  • Slow drains in several fixturesA main line problem, not a single clog.
  • Gurgling from toilets and tubsAir is trapped behind a restriction.
  • Sewage smell in the yard or basementA crack lets gas escape.
  • Wet or sunken patches over the lineWaste is leaking into the soil.
  • Repeat backups after cleaningThe pipe has a defect that catches waste.
  • Foundation cracks near the lineSoil washed out around a leak.

Camera First, Then Decide

We run a camera through the line before quoting. The footage shows a root intrusion, a crack, a belly where water pools or a joint that has separated. You see the same picture we do.

The location and type of damage decide the repair. A localized crack with root intrusion often suits a spot repair. A long run of damage suits lining or replacement. A fully collapsed pipe needs excavation.

Repair Options Compared

Each option fits a different kind of damage.

OptionWhat it isFits
Spot repairCut out and replace a short sectionA localized crack or offset.
Pipe lining (trenchless)A resin liner cured inside the old pipeCracks and roots over a longer run with sound alignment.
Pipe burstingNew pipe pulled through as the old one breaksFailed pipe that can be replaced in place.
Open trench replacementDig and lay new pipeCollapsed or badly misaligned pipe.

Trenchless methods leave lawns and driveways in place, but they need the pipe to be in a condition the method can handle. The camera decides.

Highland Rules and Utilities

The City of Highland's permit exceptions page says a permit is not required to stop leaks in drain, soil, waste or vent pipe. It also says replacing a defective concealed trap, drain pipe or vent pipe with new material counts as new work that needs a permit and inspection. Ask the Building and Safety Division which category your repair falls under.

Before any digging, call 811 so underground utilities are marked. East Valley Water District serves Highland and parts of San Bernardino, and sewer and water lines may sit close together in older streets.

Replacement sewer pipe must meet the standards in the plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the repair must be inspected where a permit is required.

Roots, Age and Soil Movement

Roots find the smallest joint and grow into it. Clay and cast iron lines have many joints, so they are common targets. Lining seals those joints from the inside.

Ground movement can also shift a pipe or open a joint. After a cleaning or a repair, ask for the camera footage so you have a record of the condition.
