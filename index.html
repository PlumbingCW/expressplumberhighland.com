Local Water Data

How Hard Is Highland Water?

East Valley Water District publishes an annual Consumer Confidence Report, and the 2026 edition lists average hardness at 152 mg/L. By a standard conversion of 17.1 mg/L per grain, that is about 8.9 grains per gallon. The USGS scale places that in the hard range.

Hard water leaves scale on faucets and glass, and it builds inside water heaters and pipes. If your fixtures crust over or your heater is noisy, hardness is a likely cause.

Scale shortens the life of heaters, cartridges and valves, so a flush and a softener can pay off. Our water softener and filtration page explains the options, and water heater repair covers scale related failures.

Plumbing in a Citrus Town at the Foot of the Mountains

Highland sits at the base of the San Bernardino Mountains, and its older streets near the Highland Historic District on Palm Avenue grew up around citrus groves. The pipe material in any home depends on when it was plumbed and what was repaired since, so a camera look at the drain and a pressure test of the supply side show whether spot repairs are enough or whether repiping is worth planning.

The City of Highland Building and Safety Division is the permit authority, and its permit exceptions page lists work that does not need a permit. East Valley Water District serves the city. Newer subdivisions farther east, such as East Highlands Ranch, were built under the City's planned unit development rules, and each has its own neighborhood page. Tell the person who answers how old your home is so the right tools are discussed.
