Faucet Repair in Highland, CA

Drips, weak flow and leaks under the sink, repaired with the right cartridge, washer or full replacement.

Call (909) 505-3225All Services

Faucet repair in Highland, CA fixes drips, weak flow, loose handles and leaks under the sink. A steady drip wastes water and can stain a basin, and a leak under a cabinet can damage the floor, so it is worth acting early.

Ask about timing when you call. If a faucet is spraying or a supply line is leaking, close the small valve under the sink and call us.

What Your Faucet Is Telling You

Different symptoms point to different parts.

  • Drip from the spoutA worn cartridge, washer or seat.
  • Leak at the base of the handleA worn O-ring or packing.
  • Water under the sinkA loose or cracked supply line, or a leaking trap.
  • Weak flowA clogged aerator or scale in the cartridge.
  • Hot and cold mixA failing cartridge or valve.
  • Loose or stiff handleWorn parts or mineral buildup.

Repair Versus Replace

A cartridge or washer is a low-cost repair on a faucet that is otherwise in good shape. A faucet with a corroded body, a pitted finish or parts that are no longer made is cheaper to replace than to chase.

We tell you which case you have and give both prices when the answer is close. If you already have a new faucet, we can install it and test the lines.

Hard Water and Faucets in Highland

East Valley Water District reports average hardness of 152 mg/L in 2026, which leaves white scale on spouts and inside aerators. Scale narrows the flow and wears seals, so faucets in hard water areas often need work sooner.

Unscrewing the aerator and soaking it in vinegar often restores flow. If the problem comes back within a few weeks, the cartridge may be scaled. A softener reduces the problem, and our water softener page explains the options.

California also sets flow standards for faucets, according to state water efficiency rules, so a replacement faucet will carry a flow rating on its box.

Outdoor Faucets and Hose Bibs

A dripping hose bib wastes water and can leak inside the wall. We replace the washer or the whole bib and check for a leak at the connection.

Highland winters are mild, but a rare freeze can crack an exposed spigot. Cover bibs on cold nights and check for a slow drip in the morning.

Kitchen Faucets and Pull Down Sprayers

Kitchen faucets add a sprayer hose, a diverter and often a filtered water line. A weak spray usually means a clogged aerator or a kinked hose. A leak under the sink during spray use often points to the hose connection or the weight on the hose.

We repair the hose, replace the diverter or swap the faucet when parts are not available. If you have a filter faucet, tell us the brand so we bring the right cartridge.
