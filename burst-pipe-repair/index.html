Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Highland, CA

We stop the water, repair or replace the pipe and tell you what to document for your insurer.

Call (909) 505-3225All Services

Burst pipe repair in Highland, CA starts with stopping the water, then repairing the failed section and checking the rest of the line. A burst pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a home in an hour, so it is an emergency and you should call right away.

Shut off the main water valve now. Turn off the power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Then call us and, if you can, open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the system.

First Ten Minutes

What you do before help arrives limits the damage.

  1. Shut off the main

    Find the valve where the line enters the house, or at the meter.

  2. Drain the system

    Open the lowest faucet to release pressure.

  3. Protect electrical safety

    Stay out of standing water near outlets and the panel.

  4. Move belongings

    Lift furniture, rugs and electronics away from the water.

  5. Photograph the damage

    Take photos before you clean, for your insurer.

  6. Call us

    Give the address and tell us where the water is coming from.

Why Pipes Burst

Age and corrosion

Old galvanized steel and aging copper thin from the inside. A pinhole grows into a split under pressure.

High water pressure

Pressure above the normal range strains joints and fixtures. A pressure regulator and a gauge can show whether yours is too high.

Cold snaps

Highland rarely sees hard freezes, but exposed lines in attics and outdoor walls can freeze on an unusual night. Insulating them is an easy prevention.

Highland Water Pressure and Pipes

East Valley Water District supplies water to Highland, and pressure varies with elevation. Homes higher on the foothill streets near the San Bernardino Mountains may see higher static pressure than homes in the flat areas, and a regulator protects the pipes.

Galvanized steel supply lines corrode from the inside and often fail in clusters, so a burst on an older line is a sign to evaluate the whole line.

Replacement pipe and fittings must meet the plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and a repair raises a permit question if it changes the system.

After the Water Stops

Water damage gets worse the longer the materials stay wet. Wet drywall, insulation and flooring should be dried or removed within a couple of days to avoid mold, which can start within 24 to 48 hours.

We can point you to drying and restoration help, and we note the failed pipe and the repair so your claim has the facts. Many policies cover sudden bursts but not the pipe itself, so ask your insurer.

Preventing the Next One

Know where your main shutoff is and test it twice a year, because valves that are never turned can seize. Check static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib. A reading well above the typical home range suggests a regulator that needs attention.

Insulate exposed pipes in attics and exterior walls, replace aging braided supply lines at toilets and sinks and repair small drips before they get bigger. These steps cost little compared with a flooded room.
