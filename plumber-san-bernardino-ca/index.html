Water, Sewer and History in San Bernardino

San Bernardino was settled in 1851, and it is a large city with more than one water provider. East Valley Water District serves portions of the city as well as Highland, and its Sterling Natural Resource Center sits at 25318 5th Street. Your bill shows which agency serves your address.

Because the city has several water and sewer providers, ask which one serves you before assuming a blockage is yours. A camera at the cleanout shows whether the problem is on your side or in the public main.

San Bernardino follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and its own Building and Safety office decides what needs a permit.
