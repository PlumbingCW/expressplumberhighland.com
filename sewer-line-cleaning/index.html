Sewer line cleaning in Highland, CA clears the main line that carries all your household waste to the street, and when it blocks, every drain in the house backs up. Call about sewage backups and slow main lines.

While you wait, stop using water, do not flush and keep children and pets away from any wet floor. If you can find the cleanout, leave it closed until help arrives.

Why the Main Line Clogs

The main line sees everything: wipes, grease, hair, tree roots and paper. A short section that is heavily coated narrows the pipe, and the next flush stops there.

Roots are the leading culprit in older lines. They enter at joints seeking moisture and spread, catching paper and waste until the line stops. Cleaning removes the blockage. It does not remove the reason roots came in.

Highland has a long citrus history, and mature trees remain along many older streets near Palm Avenue. Roots are a common cause of main line clogs on those lots. A camera visit tells you whether your tree is the cause.

Cleanout access and pipe slope are set by the plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and both affect how far a cable or jetter can travel.

What To Do After a Backup

Once the line is clear, run water in each fixture to confirm flow. Clean any affected area with a disinfectant and wear gloves. Porous materials that sewage touched, such as carpet pad, often have to be discarded.

Take photos before cleanup for your insurer. Ask the plumber for a note on the cause so you have it for the claim.
