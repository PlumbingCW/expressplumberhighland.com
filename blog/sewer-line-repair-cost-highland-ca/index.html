Sewer Line Repair Cost: A Price Guide for Highland, CA

By the Highland Express Plumbing team.Published November 13, 2026.Updated November 13, 2026.

Sewer line repair cost in Highland depends on the method, the length of damage, the depth of the pipe and what sits on top of it. A short spot repair costs far less than a full replacement under a driveway, and a camera inspection tells you which one you need before you spend anything.

We do not quote a number without scoping the line, because the range is wide. This guide explains what drives the price so you can compare quotes fairly.

The Five Things That Set the Price

Every sewer quote is built from the same few pieces: the method, the length, the depth, the surface restoration and the permit. Two contractors can quote the same pipe very differently if one assumes a trench and the other assumes lining.

Ask each contractor to name the method, the footage and what restoration is included. A quote that says only sewer repair with one number is hard to compare.

Repair Methods and What They Cost You

The method changes both the price and how much of your property is disturbed.

MethodHow it worksCost pattern
Spot repairDig and replace one short sectionLowest, if the damage is limited.
Pipe liningA resin liner cured inside the old pipeMid range, little digging.
Pipe burstingNew pipe pulled through as the old breaksMid to high, limited digging.
Open trench replacementDig and lay new pipeHighest, includes restoration.

Lining and bursting need the pipe to be in a condition they can handle, so the camera decides what is possible.

Cost Drivers You Can See Before the Quote

Camera inspection

A scope shows the damage, its location and its length. It is a small cost that avoids a large guess. See sewer inspection.

Depth and access

A line two feet down is cheaper to reach than one at six feet. A line under a driveway or a slab costs more to open and more to restore.

Pipe condition around the damage

A crack with sound pipe on both sides can be repaired in one spot. A long belly or repeated cracks may mean replacing more.

Permits and Utilities in Highland

The City of Highland permit exceptions page says a permit is not required to stop leaks in drain, soil, waste or vent pipe. It also says replacing a defective concealed trap, drain pipe or vent pipe with new material counts as new work that needs a permit. A permit adds a fee and an inspection to the schedule, so ask which category your repair falls in.

East Valley Water District serves Highland, and the public sewer main is in the street. The line from your house to that main is generally your responsibility. A camera at the cleanout shows whether a blockage sits on your side or in the main.

The plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), sets standards for the pipe used in a sewer repair, and those standards are part of why a repair needs an inspection.

Does Insurance Cover Sewer Repair?

Standard homeowner policies often exclude damage from tree roots and normal wear to a sewer line. Some insurers sell a service line endorsement, and some cover resulting water damage from a sudden backup. Read your policy or ask your agent before you approve work.

Keep the camera footage and the written findings. They support a claim if your policy covers any part of the work.

Questions to Ask Before You Approve Work

Ask whether the quote includes the camera, the permit, the inspection and the restoration of your yard or driveway. Ask what happens if the dig finds more damage than the camera showed. Ask how long the work will take and whether you can use water during it.

Also ask how the repair is warrantied in writing. A clear answer on each question helps you compare two quotes on the same terms, and it shows you which contractor planned the job and which one is guessing.

A Realistic Look at Timing

A spot repair can take a day. Lining takes about a day for prep and cure. A full trench replacement can take several days, plus restoration. Permit and inspection scheduling can add time.

Ask for a schedule in writing. If your home is rented or you run a business at the address, timing matters as much as price.

How to Compare Quotes

Ask for the same three things from every contractor: the method, the footage and what is included in restoration. Ask whether a permit and inspection are in the price. Ask whether the price changes if the dig reveals more damage.

A fair quote also tells you what happens if the camera finds something unexpected. We explain the options on our sewer line repair page, and this site covers San Bernardino and Redlands as well as Highland.
