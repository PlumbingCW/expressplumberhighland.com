What This Site Is

Highland Express Plumbing is a plumbing information site and call line for Highland, California. It also covers Redlands, San Bernardino and Mentone.

The site does not post reviews, awards, years in business or license numbers, because none of those have been verified. It publishes general plumbing information, local facts from public sources and a phone number you can call to ask about service.

What to Ask When You Call

Ask who is answering the phone and who will do the work. Ask for the plumber license details and check them yourself, because a license can be looked up in minutes. The Contractors State License Board has a public tool for checking a contractor.

Ask for the price, and whether an after-hours rate applies, before work begins. Ask how soon someone can arrive, and treat any answer as an estimate until it is confirmed.

If water is flooding a room, close the main water valve first. It is usually at the meter or on the street side of the house, and finding it before an emergency saves time when minutes matter.

Why Local Facts Matter in Highland

Highland water is hard, with average hardness of 152 mg/L in East Valley Water District's 2026 report, and scale shortens the life of heaters and fixtures. Knowing that helps you judge advice about softeners, flushing and heater replacement.

The City of Highland Building and Safety Division sets permit rules and publishes bulletins, including the April 2017 Water Heater Installation Bulletin. The guides on this site point to those documents so you can read them yourself.

What Happens When You Call

Expect to be asked where you are, what is happening, whether water is still running and whether anyone is in danger. If there is a gas smell, a fire or an electrical hazard near water, leave the building and call emergency services first.

Then ask what help is available for your address and what it would cost. Write down the name of the person you speak with, the price you are quoted and any arrival estimate. A sewage backup, a water heater failure and a hidden leak each need different equipment, so describe the symptom as clearly as you can.

Our Service Area

This site covers Highland and nearby places, including Redlands, San Bernardino and Mentone. The areas share older neighborhoods, newer subdivisions and a mix of water and sewer providers, so the first question is often which agency serves your address.

East Valley Water District serves Highland and portions of San Bernardino. Redlands runs its own water and sewer service. Mentone has more than one provider, depending on the street. Knowing your provider tells you who to call if the problem is at the meter or in the street.

Services Covered

The site has a page for drains, sewers, water heaters, leaks, pipes, fixtures and water quality. The full list is on the services page, and each page explains what the job involves, what changes the price and what to do before help arrives.

There is also a page on commercial plumbing for restaurants, offices, retail and rental buildings.

How the Guides Are Written

The blog guides use the water district report, the City of Highland permit exceptions page and the city bulletins for local facts. Where a rule could not be confirmed from a primary source, the guide says to ask the agency or test your tap.

The guides are general information. They are not a substitute for an on-site look, and a plumber who can see your pipes will always give better advice than a web page. If something in a guide does not match what you see at home, ask a licensed plumber.

How to Check a Plumber Before You Hire

Ask for the contractor license number and look it up on the Contractors State License Board website. Confirm that the license is active and that its classification covers plumbing work. Ask for proof of insurance and ask who will actually do the work.

Ask for a written contract that states the scope, the price and the payment schedule. California requires a written contract for most home improvement work above a small dollar amount. If anyone pressures you to pay in cash or sign on the spot, stop and call someone else.

What This Site Will Not Claim

This site does not quote prices it cannot back, promise arrival times or show license numbers you cannot verify. If you are told something on a call that sounds like a guarantee, ask for it in writing.
