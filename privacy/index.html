What This Site Collects

This site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email or phone number through the site itself.

Like any website, the host records basic technical data in server logs, such as the pages requested, the browser type and the time. That data is used to run and protect the site.

Phone Calls

When you call (909) 505-3225, your call may be answered through a call handling service. Your phone number and what you say on the call are used to respond to your request and to follow up on your request.

If a call is recorded, you will be told at the start. We do not sell your information.

Email

If you email contact@expressplumberhighland.com, we use your message to reply. We keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

Third Party Content

Pages include an embedded Google Map. Loading it sends your browser to Google, which has its own privacy policy. The map loads only when you scroll near it.
