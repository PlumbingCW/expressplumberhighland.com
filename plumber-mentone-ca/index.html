Emergency Plumbing in Mentone

Mentone sits between Highland and Yucaipa at the foot of the mountains, and its homes include older properties on larger lots and newer houses on smaller streets. Larger lots often mean longer service lines and, in some cases, septic or private systems.

When you call, tell us whether your property is on city sewer or septic. That decides whether the problem is a main line, a septic tank or a private line, and which tool we bring.

When to Call Right Away From Mentone

These situations should not wait.

  • Sewage or gray water coming up in the homeThe line or system is blocked.
  • A wet patch in the yardA service line may be leaking.
  • A spinning meter with everything offA hidden leak is running.
  • No hot waterThe heater has failed.
  • Low pressure at every fixtureA supply line may be damaged.
  • A burst or spraying pipeWater is entering the home.

Water Lines, Septic and Mill Creek

Mentone is near Mill Creek, which drains the mountains above, and homes on larger lots may have long buried water lines from the meter to the house. A long service line has more joints and more chances to leak, so a wet patch or sudden drop in pressure deserves a quick test. See water line repair.

Water service in the Mentone area can come from more than one provider, so check your bill before you call the utility about a problem at the meter. If your property uses septic, tell the person who answers at the start.

Plumbing in Mentone follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and county rules apply to permits and septic work.

Permits and Rules in Mentone

Because Mentone is unincorporated, permits for building work are handled by San Bernardino County and not by a city office. Septic work has its own county rules. We explain what applies before work starts.

Call 811 before any digging. On larger lots, utility lines can run long distances and cross property boundaries.

Planning Ahead for Rural Style Addresses

Some Mentone streets curve or dead end, and a navigation app may send a truck to the wrong gate. Tell the person who answers a landmark and say whether your driveway is long or shared.

If you can, turn on an outdoor light and leave the gate open. At night those small steps save minutes, and minutes matter when water is running.
